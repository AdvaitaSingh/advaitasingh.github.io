Dealers of Direct Democracy: Strategic Initiation, Popularity and Agenda-Setting in Top-Down Referendums

BAFFI Master Theses Working Paper Series · 2025

This paper develops a theoretical model of the strategic incentives of incumbent politicians to initiate referendums. It examines the tension between removing disadvantageous issues from electoral competition and gaining popularity through referendum victories, and shows how these incentives can diverge from voter welfare.
